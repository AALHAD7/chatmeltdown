Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

This policy covers chatmeltdown-poster, a private tool used only by the Chat Meltdown team to publish videos to the team's own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

What data the tool accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool requests permission to upload videos to that YouTube channel (YouTube Data API, youtube.upload scope). It uses this only to upload videos the owner chooses and to set their title and description. It does not read, collect or store any other Google user data, and it does not access other people's accounts.

How data is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are never sent to any server other than Google's and Meta's official APIs.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Chat Meltdown YouTube channel or Instagram page.
